The founder

Tunde Atayeshe Owolabi

Nigerian originator of SOVPOL — Sovereign Police, its 39-Article Charter and the Sovereign Cooperative Security Architecture, offered to governments for consideration.

Write to himRead his White Paper
Cooperation without subordination.

His proposition is that sovereign states should not have to surrender lawful national authority to cooperate effectively across borders.

He prepared the SOVPOL Foundational Policy White Paper, presented on 12 July 2026, as Convener and Initiator. It sets out the Sovereign Cooperative Security Architecture, the pathway through government consultation, and, plainly, what SOVPOL does not yet have: registration, members, recognition or police powers.

The framework he proposes is built on consent, law and accountability. Its Charter limits even his own designation.

In his words

SOVPOL facilitates sovereign-to-sovereign police cooperation, complementing without replacing the authority of national police forces.

From a statement originally in Spanish, translated here.

SOVPOL will remain without operational functions until legitimate governmental authorisations and appropriate institutional mechanisms are established.

The same statement adds that participating states keep their sovereignty and that the proposed General Assembly would be the principal deliberative body under the Charter.

The limits his Charter sets on his own role

The proposed designation identifies the Founding Custodian of the Charter’s vision and principles. Under the Charter it is not:

Personal ownership

SOVPOL does not belong to its founder.

Hereditary sovereignty

The role does not pass by inheritance.

Unlimited executive authority

No executive office exceeds what the Charter confers.

Perpetual operational command

Authority is bounded and accountable.

The Charter adds that SOVPOL’s constitutional continuity and independence survive the tenure of any founder, president or office-holder.

Let no power become greater than the law, and let no law become empty of humanity.
